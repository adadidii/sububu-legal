Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Who we are

Sububu is an app made by Difira Studio ("we", "us"). It helps you keep track of free trials and subscriptions, reminds you before you are charged, and helps you cancel or ask for a refund. If you have questions about this policy, write to support@sububu.app.

The short version

  • Sububu has no account and no server. Your subscription list stays on your phone.
  • Reading notifications (auto-detect) is optional and happens only on your phone.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device’s advertising ID. Sububu Pro has no ads.
  • We never sell your data.

What stays on your phone

Everything you put into Sububu is stored only on your phone, in the app’s private storage: your trials and subscriptions (names, prices, dates, notes), reminders, money saved, refund requests and your settings. We cannot see this information, and it is not sent to us or to anyone else.

If you uninstall Sububu or use Settings → Privacy → Delete all data, this information is erased from your phone.

Auto-detect and notification access

Auto-detect is off until you turn it on. Before you do, Sububu explains what it reads, and Android asks you to allow notification access.

When it is on, Sububu reads the text of notifications on your phone to spot payment receipts from known subscription services (for example a Google Play or bank receipt). This happens only on your phone:

  • Notifications that are not a receipt from a known service are discarded right away and never stored.
  • When a receipt matches, only the service name, price and date are saved, on your phone.
  • Nothing from your notifications is sent to us or to anyone else.

You can turn auto-detect off at any time in Settings → Privacy, and remove notification access in your phone’s settings.

Installed apps

To show real app icons and to help auto-detect, Sububu checks whether some known subscription apps (about 150, such as streaming and music apps) are installed. It cannot see any other apps. This check happens only on your phone, and you can turn it off in Settings → Privacy.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows one ad, in the Money tab, from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device’s advertising ID, your approximate location (based on your IP address), basic device information and your interactions with the ad. Google handles this information under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Sububu asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.

Your subscription list is never shared with Google or any advertiser. Sububu Pro has no ads, and Sububu does not request ads for Pro users.

Purchases

Sububu Pro is bought through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment; we never see your card or bank details. Sububu receives a confirmation that you bought Pro and keeps it on your phone so it can unlock Pro features.

Usage statistics

Sububu does not collect usage statistics or analytics. If we add anonymous usage statistics in the future, they will be off unless you turn them on in Settings → Privacy, and we will update this policy first.

When you write to us

Sububu never sends e-mails for you. When you contact support or ask a service for a refund, Sububu opens a draft in your own e-mail app, and you decide whether to send it.

If you write to us, we receive what you send: your e-mail address, your message and, only if you leave “Include device info” on, the app version, your plan (Free or Pro), your Android version, phone model, whether notifications are allowed and whether auto-detect is on. Your subscription list is never included. We use this only to answer you, and we delete it when it is no longer needed for that.

Refund e-mails go from your e-mail app to the service you choose. That service handles them under its own privacy policy.

Backup files

Back up & restore saves one file with your Sububu data wherever you choose (for example your phone’s Downloads folder or a cloud drive). The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe. If you store it with a cloud service, that service’s privacy policy applies to the file.

Children

Sububu is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

  • Turn auto-detect and the installed-apps check on or off in Settings → Privacy.
  • Change your ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy choices (where it applies), or get Pro for no ads.
  • Erase your Sububu data with Settings → Privacy → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app. (Pro stays linked to your Google account.)
  • Ask us to delete e-mails you sent us by writing to support@sububu.app.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information about you. Because your Sububu data stays on your phone, you can see, change and delete it in the app at any time. For anything else, contact us.

Security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage, protected by Android. No one else can read it through Sububu. Please keep your phone locked and your backup files safe.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For important changes, we will let you know in the app or in the update notes.

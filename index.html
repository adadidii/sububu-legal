FAQ

Good to know

How do I cancel a subscription on Google Play?

Open the Play Store, tap your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, choose the subscription and tap Cancel subscription. Uninstalling the app does not cancel it. Sububu shows these steps, plus the cancel page for 150+ services, right when you need them.

How do I stop a free trial from charging me?

Cancel before the trial ends; you usually keep access until the last day. Add the trial to Sububu when you start it and you'll be reminded 3 days before, 1 day before and on the morning it bills.

Money was taken for a subscription I forgot. What now?

Check Payments & subscriptions in the Play Store and your bank or e-wallet app to see who charged you. Cancel it first so it doesn't happen again, then ask Google Play or the service for a refund. Sububu tells you where to ask and drafts the request.

Does Sububu cancel subscriptions for me?

No. Sububu reminds you in time and shows the exact steps to cancel in the service's own app or website. Only you can cancel, so you stay in control.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no server. Everything you add stays on your phone. You can back it up to a file yourself.

What does "finds them for you" read?

It's optional and Android-only. Sububu checks which known subscription apps are installed and looks for payment receipts in your notifications, on your phone only. Anything that isn't a receipt is ignored and never stored. You confirm every item before it's added and can turn it off anytime.

Will I get my money back?

That's up to the store or the service. Sububu helps you ask the right place with a polite, ready-to-send email draft, and reminds you to follow up.

Will reminders still arrive after I restart my phone?

Yes. Reminders are rescheduled after a restart, a time-zone change and app updates. On some phones, battery savers can delay notifications; Sububu shows you how to allow them and lets you send a test reminder.

Is there an iPhone version?

Android comes first. An iOS version is planned for later.
